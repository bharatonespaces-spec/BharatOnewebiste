About Box Office Jasai

Located near Dastan Fata on JNPT Road, Box Office Jasai sits at the strategic convergence of India's newest international airport and Asia's busiest container port. With the Navi Mumbai International Airport (NMIA) now operating 24/7 commercial flights, Jasai has officially become the primary logistics and transit artery of the entire Mumbai Metropolitan Region.

Our fully furnished offices are designed for professionals who need to be where the action is — connected to Mumbai in 20 minutes via Atal Setu (MTHL), Navi Mumbai in 10 minutes, and with the Jasai Railway Station just 900 metres away.

Key Highlights:

  • ✓ 12 min from NMIA Terminal 1 — India's newest international airport

  • ✓ Near Dastan Fata, directly on JNPT Road with Atal Setu (MTHL) exit access
